**SERVER RULES**
This server is for Warden players. By joining, you agree to these rules.

**1. Access**
1.1 One person: one Discord account and one Steam profile.
1.2 For access, the bot checks your linked accounts and asks for a screenshot of your faction choice. This data is used only for verification and is kept while you are on the server. Refusing verification means access is denied.
1.3 When a new war starts, you must confirm your faction again.
1.4 Do not hand your access to others, share invites, or post screenshots of closed channels. Only the bot creates invites.

**2. Security**
2.1 Stockpile codes, muster coordinates and operation plans go only in closed channels and operation cards. In open channels, an autofilter deletes six-digit codes.
2.2 Do not forward material from closed channels outside the server.
2.3 If you notice a suspicious player, message the moderators and do not take matters into your own hands. Suspicion is not proof.

**3. Operations**
3.1 The organizer is responsible for the card: requirements, vehicles, supplies, roles. When changing the terms, they note it in the card.
3.2 If you sign up, show up or cancel in advance. After three no-shows in a row, your sign-ups are closed for 7 days.
3.3 During an operation, follow the organizer's instructions. Disputed decisions are discussed afterwards, in the after-action report.
