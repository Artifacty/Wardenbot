**4. Listings and recruitment**
4.1 Trade only in-game materials and vehicles. Selling for real money, trading accounts and paid services outside the game are forbidden.
4.2 A listing states what, how much and where to hand it over. After the deal, mark the listing closed.
4.3 Cheating in a deal: a ban from the listings section, and a complaint to the moderators with evidence.
4.4 Regiment recruitment is run by commanders and officers of the regiment. One post per 7 days, no spam in private messages.

**5. Conduct**
5.1 Respect each other. Insults, harassment, discrimination and hate: a ban without warning.
5.2 Politics outside the game, adult content and ads for other servers are forbidden.
5.3 Write in Russian or English. In paired channels, use your own language and the bot will translate. Machine translation makes mistakes, so ask when in doubt.
5.4 Cheats, exploits and bypassing the game's rules are not discussed.
5.5 Settle conflicts between regiments in the game or through commanders, not in the general channels.

**6. Penalties**
6.1 Order: warning, mute for up to 7 days, ban. Espionage, fraud, hate and cheating mean an immediate ban.
6.2 All decisions are recorded in the moderation log. You can appeal through the bot, and you will get a reply within 3 days.
6.3 The rules may change. Changes are published in the news channel.
